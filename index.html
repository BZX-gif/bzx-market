Frequently Asked Questions
How does hiring a player work?▼
Click "Hire Now" on any player card. Fill in your IGN, tournament, and WhatsApp number. You'll be connected to the player via WhatsApp within seconds — completely free, zero commission.
What is Pro Boost and is ₹29/month worth it?▼
Pro Boost pins your card above all free players, adds an animated cyan border, a 🔵 Pro Verified badge, online status dot, and a hire counter. Pro Boosted players get 3× more hire requests on average. At ₹1/day, one hire easily covers 1–2 months of cost.
Is my payment secure?▼
All payments are made via UPI directly. We never store payment details. Listing fees are collected via WhatsApp confirmation before your card goes live — no surprise charges, ever.
How fast does my card go live after paying?▼
Pro Boost and Elite cards go live within 30 minutes of UPI confirmation during active hours (9 AM – 11 PM). Legendary cards may take up to 1 hour due to custom design. Free cards appear instantly after submission.
Can I cancel Pro Boost anytime?▼
Yes. Send a WhatsApp message before your next renewal date and your card is cancelled with no questions asked. You keep the boost until your current month ends. No annual contracts, ever.
What if a player doesn't respond after I hire them?▼
All players on BZX are real and have provided their active WhatsApp number during listing. If a player doesn't respond within 2 hours, contact us via the BZX WhatsApp support line and we'll assist you in finding an alternative match.
How do I get my Pro Boost card looking different from the example?▼
When you submit your Pro Boost request, include your IGN, your actual K/D and Win Rate, your role, up to 4 skill tags, and your WhatsApp number. Our team will build a card matching your real stats. The animated border, verified badge, and pin placement are automatic.
