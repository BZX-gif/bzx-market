04 Cancellation Policy

You may cancel a booking at any time before the session begins. Cancellation requests made before a player accepts your booking request are fully refunded.

Once a player has confirmed and the session has begun, cancellations are not eligible for a refund. If the player fails to deliver after confirmation, BZX will intervene and process a full refund on your behalf.

✅ Cancel Before Session Starts = Full Refund If you change your mind before the player accepts your booking request, or the player has not yet begun the agreed service, you are entitled to a complete refund with no deductions.
05 Non-Refundable Situations

The following circumstances are explicitly excluded from our refund policy:

🚫 Refunds Will NOT Be Issued For: • Sessions fully completed and delivered as described
• Poor in-game performance (win/loss results are not guaranteed)
• Buyer's failure to join at the agreed time
• Losses due to buyer's own gameplay decisions
• Disputes raised more than 48 hours after the transaction
• Purchases made under a promotional or discounted offer (unless platform error)
• Violations of BZX Terms of Service by the buyer

Game outcomes (wins, losses, rank push results) are skill-based and cannot be guaranteed. Refunds are based on service delivery, not match results.

06 Payment Disputes & Chargebacks

If you initiate a chargeback with your bank or card issuer without first contacting BZX support, your account may be suspended pending investigation. We work closely with Razorpay to resolve disputes fairly and transparently.

We strongly encourage buyers to contact our support team first — most issues are resolved within 24–48 hours without the need for a formal dispute.

⚠️ Please Contact Us First Filing a chargeback without first reaching out to BZX support may result in a temporary account hold. Our team resolves 95%+ of valid payment issues within 24 hours.
