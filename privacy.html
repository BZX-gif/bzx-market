🔒 Privacy

Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we keep it safe on BZX Pro Roster.

Last Updated: May 2025  ·  Version 1.0
01 Information We Collect

When you use BZX Pro Roster, we collect the following types of information to provide and improve our services:

  • Account Information: Your in-game name (IGN), email address, password (encrypted), and selected role.
  • Profile Data: Statistics you publish on your card such as K/D, win rate, skills, tier, and per-match price.
  • Transaction Data: Order ID, amount, date, and status of payments processed via Razorpay. We do not store card numbers, UPI PINs, or bank credentials.
  • Communication Data: Messages exchanged between players and clients through the in-app messaging system.
  • Technical Data: Browser type, device information, IP address, and basic usage analytics (page views, button clicks).
🔒 What We DO NOT Collect We do not collect your government ID, location data, social media contacts, or any biometric information. We never ask for your UPI PIN, card CVV, or banking password.
02 How We Use Your Information

The data we collect is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • To create and maintain your BZX Pro Roster account
  • To display your player profile/card to other users searching the roster
  • To process payments securely through Razorpay and send confirmations
  • To facilitate booking requests, sessions, and refund handling
  • To communicate important platform updates, security notices, or policy changes
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or violations of our Terms of Service
  • To analyse aggregated, anonymous usage trends to improve the platform
03 Payment Data & Razorpay

All payments on BZX Pro Roster are processed by Razorpay Payment Solutions Pvt. Ltd., a licensed payment aggregator regulated by the Reserve Bank of India. Payment data is collected, stored, and processed entirely by Razorpay on PCI-DSS compliant infrastructure.

BZX receives only a transaction reference (Order ID, status, and amount) for record-keeping. We do not see or store your card number, expiry date, CVV, UPI PIN, or net-banking credentials.

🔒 PCI-DSS Compliant Payment Handling Your payment data is governed by Razorpay's Privacy Policy. We strongly recommend reviewing Razorpay's policy at razorpay.com/privacy for complete details on how they handle financial data.
04 Cookies & Tracking

BZX Pro Roster uses essential cookies and browser storage for the following purposes:

  • Session cookies: Keep you logged in across pages.
  • Preference storage: Remember your sort, filter, and theme settings.
  • Anonymous analytics: Cloudflare Web Analytics measures site performance with no personal identifiers.

We do not use third-party advertising cookies, pixel trackers, or cross-site tracking technologies.

05 Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to advertisers or marketing companies. We share limited data only with the following trusted service providers, strictly for operating the platform:

  • Razorpay — for processing payments (governed by Razorpay's Privacy Policy).
  • Supabase — secure cloud database that stores your account and profile.
  • Cloudflare — content delivery and DDoS protection.
  • Legal authorities — only if required by Indian law or a valid court order.
06 Your Rights

As a user of BZX Pro Roster, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Update inaccurate information from your dashboard at any time.
  • Deletion: Request permanent deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Withdraw consent: Stop using the platform at any time without penalty.
  • Portability: Receive your profile data in a structured, machine-readable format.
✅ How to Exercise Your Rights Contact us via Instagram @blazezonexofficial with your registered email. We respond to all data-related requests within 7 working days.
07 Data Retention

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations:

  • Active accounts: data retained while the account remains active.
  • Inactive accounts (12+ months): notification sent before automatic deactivation.
  • Transaction records: retained for 7 years as required by Indian tax and accounting laws.
  • Deletion requests: processed within 30 days, except for transaction records mandated by law.
08 Security Measures

BZX takes reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including:

  • HTTPS encryption on every page and API request
  • Passwords stored as one-way hashes (never in plain text)
  • Row-level security policies on our Supabase database
  • Cloudflare DDoS and bot-protection layers
  • PCI-DSS compliant payment processing through Razorpay

While we work hard to protect your information, no internet service can be 100% secure. Please use a strong, unique password and never share your login details.

09 Children's Privacy

BZX Pro Roster is intended for users aged 13 and above. Users below 18 must have parental or guardian consent before completing paid transactions. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such data, please contact us immediately for prompt removal.

10 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable laws. Significant changes will be communicated through the platform or our official social channels. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

  • Instagram: @blazezonexofficial
  • Through the in-app message system on your BZX Dashboard
